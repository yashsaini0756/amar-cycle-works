Frequently Asked Questions

Are these lights waterproof?
Yes! All our cycle lights are IPX4 rated — safe to use in rain. Rechargeable lights have sealed USB ports for extra protection.
How long does the battery last?
Rechargeable lights typically last 4-10 hours on a single charge depending on the mode used. Battery operated lights last 20-40 hours.
Will the light fit my cycle?
Yes, our lights use universal clip-on mounts that fit all cycle handlebars and seat posts.
