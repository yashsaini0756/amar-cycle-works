Frequently Asked Questions

Will the bell fit my cycle?
Yes! Our bells have universal clamps that fit all standard handlebar sizes. Free fitting at our shop.
Are brass bells better than steel?
Brass bells have a more classic, richer sound and premium feel. Steel bells are more affordable. Both work perfectly.
Do you have bells for kids cycles?
Yes! We have colorful bells with cartoon designs (Spider-Man, Doraemon, etc.) especially for kids' cycles.
How loud is the electric horn?
Electric horns produce 80-100 dB sound, much louder than regular bells. Ideal for busy roads.
