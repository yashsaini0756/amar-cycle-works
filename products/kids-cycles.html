Frequently Asked Questions

Which size cycle is right for my child?
Size depends on age and height. 12" for 2-4 yrs, 14" for 4-5 yrs, 16" for 5-7 yrs, 20" for 7-10 yrs. Bring your child to our shop for a free fit check.
Should I buy training wheels?
Training wheels are great for beginners (age 2-5). Once your child learns balance, we can remove them (free of cost).
Do you deliver kids cycles?
Yes! Free doorstep delivery within Jaipur. We also assemble it for you before delivery.
Are kids cycles safe for 3-year-olds?
Yes, 12" kids cycles are designed for 2-4-year-olds. Features rounded edges, sturdy build, and easy-to-use brakes.
