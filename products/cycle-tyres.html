Frequently Asked Questions

Which tyre size fits my cycle?
It depends on your cycle type. MTB uses 26"/27.5"/29", road cycles use 700c, kids cycles use 12"-20". Bring your cycle or WhatsApp a photo — we'll tell you the exact size.
How often should I replace my cycle tyre?
Typically every 1-2 years with regular use. If you see cracks or frequent punctures, replace immediately.
