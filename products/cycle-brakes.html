Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my brakes need replacing?
Signs include: squeaking noise, longer stopping distance, brake lever feels loose, or pad thickness less than 2mm. Free brake check at our shop!
V-brake vs Disc brake — which is better?
V-brakes are lighter and cheaper, good for city riding. Disc brakes offer superior stopping power, especially in wet conditions and off-roading. We can help you choose.
Do you service hydraulic disc brakes?
Yes! We service both mechanical and hydraulic disc brakes including fluid top-up and bleeding.
What's the brake pad replacement cost?
V-brake pads start at ₹150/pair, disc brake pads at ₹300/pair. Fitting charges may apply.
